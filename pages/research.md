## ProPStar

**Probing the Physics of Star Formation**

I am leading a survey mapping nearby star-forming regions with high angular resolution. In the first stage, we mapped the South-East region of NGC 1333 and Barnard 5, combining NOEMA and 30-m observations to recover all scales in the 3-mm band.

## Assembling the Star- and Planet-Formation Puzzle

I work on understanding star- and planet-forming systems using observations, new analysis techniques, and systematic studies of large datasets. A recurring focus is kinematics across different molecular tracers.
